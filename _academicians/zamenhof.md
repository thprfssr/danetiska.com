**Ludwik Lejzer Zamenhof** (1859–1917) was the creator of Esperanto. A Jewish physician raised in multilingual Białystok, he grew up among communities divided by language and religion. His hope was that a language belonging equally to everyone might make it easier for people to meet one another.

In 1887 he published the first account of his international language under the name *Doktoro Esperanto*. The pseudonym became the name of the language itself. Zamenhof continued to develop its literature through original writing and translation, while its growing community took up the work of using it.

Esperanto had a practical purpose, but making it usable required more than a grammar and a vocabulary. It needed correspondence, conversation, books, and people willing to make it their own.

The Danetian Academy honors Zamenhof for that achievement. He gave others a language to share, and trusted them to help it grow.
