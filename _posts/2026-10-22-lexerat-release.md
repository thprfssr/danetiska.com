The Danetian Academy has just released
[Lexerat](https://github.com/elementalbasis/lexerat),
a small
[Python](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Python_(programming_language))
tool for turning plain-text lexicons into neatly-formatted dictionaries in
[HTML](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HTML)
and
[LaTeX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LaTeX).

Lexerat lets you keep a lexicon in a plain text that can be
edited with any text editor. Simple notation organizes lemmas, definitions,
etymologies, parts of speech, and other lexicographical information into a
human-readable and machine-readable format.

Moreover, Lexerat accomplishes a very difficult task: reversing a
one-way dictionary. Many
[conlang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructed_language) dictionaries are
only kept as Conlang-English dictionaries. Lexerat makes
it possible to take this one-way dictionary and reverse it into an
English-Conlang dictionary, thereby creating a two-way dictionary.

Originally developed for the
[Dictionary of the Danetian language](/dictionary), Lexerat can be used with
any language. It is has been released as
[free software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_open-source_software)
under the
[MIT License](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_License).
