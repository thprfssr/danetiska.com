The Danetian Academy has launched the
[**Kustendja Polyglot Bible**](/bible), an online resource bringing
together the Scriptures in
[English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language),
[Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin),
[Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koine_Greek), and
[Slavonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_Slavonic)
into a unified interface.

The 
[**Kustendja Polyglot Bible**](/bible) is intended as a resource for students,
translators, and anyone interested in comparing the biblical text across the
Christian traditions. Passages may be viewed side by side, making it easier to
study the history of the text and the development of its translation.

Work is now underway to prepare the
[Danetian](/language) text for online publication.
The Academy is carefully digitizing and proofreading the historic Danetian
translation so that it can be integrated into the Kustendja Polyglot Bible
alongside the other languages. As this work progresses, the Danetian version
will gradually become available.

The Academy hopes that this resource will serve both scholars and the wider
public, while making the Scriptures more accessible to readers interested in the
literary and linguistic heritage of Danetia.
