The Danetian Academy has launched [PIESCA](https://danetiska.com/piesca/), a
Proto-Indo-European Sound Change Applier based on
[Mark Rosenfelder’s SCA2](http://www.zompist.com/sca2.html).

PIESCA combines a searchable
[Proto-Indo-European](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proto-Indo-European_language)
dictionary with a sound change applier. Users can enter their own sound changes
and apply them to every headword and recorded inflection, exploring how an
imagined descendant of Proto-Indo-European might look.

Originally developed to support work on Danetian, PIESCA may be useful to anyone
constructing an Indo-European language.
