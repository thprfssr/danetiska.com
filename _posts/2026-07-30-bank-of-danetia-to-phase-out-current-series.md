The **Bank of Danetia** (*Banca Danetzias*) has announced that it intends to
gradually phase out its current mathematician–physicist series of
banknotes in favor of a new series celebrating prominent figures from Danetian
history.

Speaking about the decision, representatives of the Bank stated:

> "It's time to incorporate Danetian figures into our currency."

The Bank added that work on the new series is already underway.

> "We're currently working on it."

Among the historical figures reportedly under consideration are:
Myron Dorostolite, Prince Hendrik I, Alexandros Krystevos, Yurik Aslan,
Dobrotitza, and Ovid, although no final designs or denominations have yet been
officially confirmed.

## The Danetian Stater

The official currency of the **Danetian Republic** (*Danetiska Respublica*) is
the **stater**, issued exclusively by the **Bank of Danetia**. Like many modern
currencies, the stater serves as the standard unit of account for prices,
salaries, taxation, savings, and everyday commerce throughout Danetia.

Current denominations include both coins and banknotes, with the latter forming
the backbone of everyday circulation.

Although the present banknote series honors renowned mathematicians and
physicists from world history, the Bank of Danetia now believes that the
nation's currency should increasingly reflect the people who shaped Danetia
itself.

## An Icon of the Stater

Perhaps the most recognizable note of the current series is the
**Euler D$1 banknote**. Featuring the Swiss mathematician Leonhard Euler, the
note has become an iconic symbol of the Danetian stater and is widely associated
with the modern visual identity of the currency.

Since its introduction, the Euler note has become one of the most recognizable
pieces of Danetian currency, appearing in educational materials, promotional
illustrations, and photographs shared both within and outside the country.

## Looking Ahead

The Bank has not yet announced a release date for the forthcoming historical
series. If adopted, the new series would represent a significant shift in the
visual identity of the stater, placing Danetian history and national heritage
at the forefront of the country's currency.
