The Danetian Academy brings together people devoted to scholarship and the
cultivation of Danetian language, history, and culture.

Its **numerary academicians** form its core circle; **corresponding academicians**
contribute research, writing, and other scholarly work; and **honorary academicians**
are recognized for their work or intellectual legacy.
