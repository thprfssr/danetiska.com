Tramontovos Ventos i Suls sjoprytiostonto, poteros esj dvomos est po kretus,
joda voiazjonts pribet ustiostos en bryni manti. Somoupsento jod prymos
redeni masjni voiazjontei apokypieni mantam iti sonts considerastos po kretus
josmod alios.

> The North Wind and the Sun were arguing which of the two was stronger, when
a traveler came dressed in a warm coat. They agreed that the first to
succeed in making the traveller take off his coat would be considered
stronger than the other.

Tramontovos Ventos blet tasme krytue jasme megst. Nu javot pleire blynut,
pleire andjue plesjet voiazjonts mantam peri sve. Tyrmeni visjosio,
Tramontovos Ventos upodot esjmektim.

> The North Wind blew as strong as he could. But the more he blew,
the more tightly did the traveler wrap his coat around himself. In the
end, the North Wind gave up the effort.

Posti tosio, Suls bryne lukeiostet, ide voiazjonts immediate djystre
apokypiostet mantam.

> After this, the Sun shone warmly, and immediately the traveller took off his
coat.

Apo tosmod, Tramontovos Ventos delgostet udvepni jod Suls est po kretus esj
dvomos.

> Therefore, the North Wind had to concede that the Sun was the stronger of the
two.
