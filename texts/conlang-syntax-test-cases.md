1. Suls lukeieti.

   > The sun shines.

2. Suls lukeieti.

   > The sun is shining.

3. Suls lukeiostet.

   > The sun shone.

4. Suls iti lukeionts.

   > The sun will shine.

5. Suls lukeieke.

   > The sun has been shining.

6. Suls lukeieti aliam vertim.

   > The sun is shining again.

7. Suls iti lukeionts usje.

   > The sun will shine tomorrow.

8. Suls lukeieti svite djystre.

   > The sun shines brightly.

9. Svita suls lukeieti.

   > The bright sun shines.

10. Suls uditi nuni.

    > The sun is rising now.

11. Visjos ludis kraugostet.

    > All the people shouted.

12. Inopoi esj ludimos kraugostont.

    > Some of the people shouted.

13. Ples ludis kraugostet dvans vertins.

    > Many of the people shouted twice.

14. Prinos ludis kraugeti plens vertins.

    > Happy people often shout.
