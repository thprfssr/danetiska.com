## Articulos 1

Visjoi sjmonovoi esties djenontor ludroi i equales en dignitati i rysjtasu.
Dotakvoses ratzione i conscientzie, delgonti sjoberomeni en prumeni bratrotas
oinoi aliomos.

> All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights. They are
endowed with reason and conscience and should act towards one another in a
spirit of brotherhood.

## Articulos 2

Visjoinos sjmon nemeti rysjtans i ludrotans provopostans en sjasmi
Declaratzioni, desj distinctzioned nipoterad djenesos, colores, sexosio,
dyndjuas, religiones, politiskas ive alias opiniones, natzionales ive sociales
origenes, economiskas positziones, djynties, ive joteras-siet alias
conditziones. En additzioni, ne iti dykmeni distinctzion fundasta en politiski,
juridiski, ive internatzionali conditzioni kisjvares ive territoriosio josmoi
prirytioit sjmon, siet independents, upo fiduciarii administratzioni, ne
somoredjonts, ive upo joteri-siet limitatzioni suveranitates.

> Everyone is entitled to all the rights and freedoms set forth in this
Declaration, without distinction of any kind, such as race, colour, sex,
language, religion, political or other opinion, national or social origin,
property, birth or other status. Furthermore, no distinction shall be made on
the basis of the political, jurisdictional or international status of the
country or territory to which a person belongs, whether it be independent,
trust, non-self-governing or under any other limitation of sovereignty.

## Articulos 3

Visjoinos individuos nemeti rysjtam ana bivam, i securitatym personas.

> Everyone has the right to life, liberty and security of person.
